This page is for the day-to-day care of a board: making one, naming it, saying how it is going, and putting it
away when the work is over.

## Create a board

1. In the sidebar, under **Projects**, click the **+** (*New project*). With no boards yet, the page asks for your
   first one straight away.
2. Type a **Project name** and click **Create**. Press Escape, or **Cancel**, to back out.
3. The board opens with its starting columns. See [How a board works](/help/basics/boards-and-columns).

A board made while you are in a team belongs to that team. A board made in your own workspace is yours alone. A board
without a name is called *Untitled project*.

## Rename a board

Click the board's name at the top of the board and type a new one; Enter saves, and so does clicking away or pressing Escape. You can also use
**...** (*Project options*) then **Rename project**, the **...** on the board's row in the sidebar then **Rename**,
or the **Name** field under **Settings**, **General**.

## The board menu

The same menu opens from every place a board is listed:

- The **...** at the top right of the board.
- The **...** on a pinned board in the sidebar, and on a board's row in the sidebar, in the portfolio table and in search
  results. It shows when you hover over the row or focus it, and always on a touch screen.
- A right-click on the board (or the context-menu key, or Shift+F10 on a focused row). On a touch screen, press and hold.

The menu holds **Rename**, **Notify me of new tasks** (or **Stop notifying me of new tasks**), **Settings**, and
**Pin to sidebar** (or **Unpin**). On the board it also lists **Show trashed** when there are trashed tasks. In the sidebar
it adds **Give it a crew**, for a team's owner or admin, and **Delete project**. A customer's board offers only pinning.

Rename from a list or search result opens the board and starts the rename there. Press Escape to close the menu; focus
goes back to where it was.

## Description and health status

Open the **Overview** tab.

- **Project description** is free text for goals, links and context. It saves when you click away, and *Saved* flashes
  once. The same field is under **Settings**, **General**. It holds up to 20,000 characters.
- **What's the status?** has three buttons: **On track**, **At risk** and **Off track**. Click the one that is selected
  to clear it. A coloured dot for the status shows next to the board's name on the *Browse projects* page.
- **Status updates** is the board's history. Choose a status, write what changed since the last update, and click
  **Post update**. The update is stamped with the status you chose and the board's status becomes that one. Your own
  updates have a bin icon to delete them. You must choose a status before you can post.

Status updates are for the team. They are not shown to customers.

## Who the board belongs to

Under **Settings**, **General**, **Owner** shows the team member the board is recorded under. It does not change who
can see the board. Only the current owner can hand the board to someone else, and a board in your own workspace has
no owner to change.

In a team, you can file a board under a customer with the button in the header that says **Team only** (your own
workspace has no such button, since it has no customers). It lists your team's
customers; pick one, or pick **Team only** to take the board back. This only decides which customer's contacts may see
the tasks you share. See [Who sees a task](/help/basics/task-visibility).

## Archive a board

Archiving is the gentle way to put a board away.

1. Open **Settings**, **General**.
2. Under **Archive**, click **Archive project**.

An archived board is hidden from the project lists and from the search results for boards, and its rules stop running.
Its tasks still turn up in search, **My tasks** and Home. The board itself still opens and its tasks can still be
edited. A banner on the board says **Archived** and has an **Unarchive** button.
To find an archived board later, open **Browse projects**: it has an **Archived** list with **Unarchive** on each row.

## Delete a board

Deleting is permanent. It removes every column, task and comment of the board, and the images in its tasks. **There is
no trash for a whole board**, and nothing can bring it back.

1. Open **Settings**, **General**, then **Delete project** under **Delete**.
2. Type the board's name exactly as shown to confirm, and click **Delete project**.

The sidebar's **...** menu also has **Delete project**; there you only confirm in a window. Any team member can delete
a board, so consider **Archive project** first. Tasks and columns you delete one at a time do go to a trash; see
[Columns and the trash](/help/basics/columns-and-trash).
