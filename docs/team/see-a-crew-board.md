A board that has a crew works in two places. The real board holds the work. The crew plans on its **crew board**: a
second board in the crew's own team, named after the real board. The crew board of *Taskmanager* is *Taskmanager (crew)*.

## Who sees a crew board

- **The team's owner** sees every crew board of the team.
- **An admin** sees the crew board of each board they manage: a board they created, or a board they are a manager of.
  An admin does not see the crew boards of other boards.
- **A member** sees only the crew boards of crews they set up.
- **Everyone else** sees none: the other members, the guests of a board and the customer contacts.

## Where to find it

On the real board, the header has a **Crew board** button before **Share**. It shows the crew board's name and how many
tasks are open on it. Click it to open the crew board.

In the board list and the sidebar, a crew board sits beside your own boards, marked **Crew board of** and the name of
the real board. You can pin it like any other board.

## What you see there

The crew board opens read-only. Its tasks, subtasks, comments and time are there to read. A banner at the top says
**The crew's board for** the real board, and **Read-only: the crew plans here.**

The server refuses every change to a crew board, whether it comes from the app or from a key. Nothing you do there
changes the crew's work or the real board.
